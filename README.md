# One Line, One Laugh

Entry for the make.fun competition
[u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3)
("One Line, One Laugh", $2, deadline 2026-11-26T03:00:00Z).

## The entry

> I told my houseplant I loved it and it died anyway — the most honest performance review I've ever received.

The line is the whole entry: a single hilarious line of at most 200 characters.
The setup is sweet (telling a plant you love it), the turn is the plant dying
anyway, and the punchline reframes the death as a deadpan corporate
"performance review" — the most honest one the speaker has ever received.
Surprise, economy and freshness: no stock joke, no setup longer than the
punchline needs.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the entry byte-for-byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
